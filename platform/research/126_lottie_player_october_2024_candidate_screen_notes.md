# Lottie Player October 2024 candidate screen

**Screen date:** 26 August 2026  
**Status:** Bounded evidence screen; no ledger admission or source-object reconstruction has occurred.

LottieFiles’ incident resolution identifies an npm-account takeover and the unauthorized publications `@lottiefiles/lottie-player@2.0.5`, `2.0.6`, and `2.0.7`. Independent Sonatype and Snyk reports identify the same finite release set and the cryptocurrency-wallet prompt behavior. This evidence is version-specific and concerns a package not in the current strict ledger. [1] [2] [3]

The next bounded step is an official npm registry metadata-only response. It may establish publication-time selection and retained pre-event object availability; it cannot establish an installation history, downstream reach, blast radius, current publisher state, or a predictive feature.
