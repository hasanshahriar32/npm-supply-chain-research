# IST final evidence and submission handoff

## What the study now supports

The primary comparison supports a paper about which applications enter documented review scope under two equally sized account portfolios. The YunoHost frame has five confirmed switches at five accounts, and a separate switch appears at 50 accounts in the exploratory analysis. The Awesome-Selfhosted evidence resolves 88 of 155 pairs. The study keeps the original comparisons and identifies each later check. This answers a bounded empirical question of interest to IST. The journal's 2026 editorial emphasizes the practical contribution, empirical validation, and transparent reporting. [Updated manuscript](../submission/ist/IST_Manuscript.pdf) · [IST editorial](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0950584926001862)

| Awesome-Selfhosted | 88 / 155 | 0 | 0–43.2% |

The final audit used the same structural-zero rule in both catalogs. All nine newer Yarn roots left by the Classic parser were checked. Two retained Yarn 4 manifests and matching version 8 root entries had development declarations only. This adds two shared negatives, leaving 67 unresolved projects in Awesome-Selfhosted. The Yarn group resolves 14 of 23 projects. The check concerns the declared root, without requiring an npm tarball match for any package. A modern Yarn cache ZIP checksum would need independent corroboration before it could establish a package artifact join. [Root audit](../research/consumer_holdout/runs/selfhosted-modern-yarn-roots/results.json) · [Yarn checksum documentation](https://yarnpkg.com/advanced/error-codes#yn0018---cache_checksum_mismatch)

Both frames still allow either direction of net change. The difference between catalogs remains unidentified even within the npm groups. A new experiment on installed or executed dependencies would address a different question and require a new design. For the submitted question, the remaining value is concentrated in checking the five confirmed witnesses and their scope. The companion contains pinned source references so either author can inspect them. [Pinned case evidence](../research/consumer_holdout/runs/yunohost-yarn-classic/confirmed_cases.md)
