\section{Validity, Limitations, and Responsible Reporting}
\textbf{Constructs.} Membership does not establish publishing authority, account independence, token scope, or control feasibility. Reach proves neither installation nor execution or secret access. Priors, hardening efficacy, and thresholds are stipulated; signer groups and bypass privileges are unobserved.

\textbf{Observation and generalization.} Collection lacks a shared field cutoff, workflow commits, and extraction-root provenance. Both graphs mix resolution contexts. Missing metadata and ambiguous zero flags can distort source sets; the high-impact-seeded sample limits npm-wide generalization.

\textbf{Internal and conclusion validity.} Independent traversals, dual incidence, toy cases, and generated values check correctness. Frozen data remain unchanged. Comparisons describe this snapshot; random orders measure selection variability. Reused orders and nested budgets make the 6,000 rows dependent.

\textbf{Boundaries and reporting.} Node.js's High-severity Brotli flaw (CVE-2026-111497) lies outside npm manifests~\cite{nodejsbrotli2026}. Source-tag identity and artifact integrity establish neither equivalent nor benign builds~\cite{litellmincident2026}. We execute no payloads or credential probes, claim no demonstrated prevention, and retain sanitized prospective metadata without emails or package bodies.
