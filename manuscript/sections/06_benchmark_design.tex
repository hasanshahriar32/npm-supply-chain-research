\section{Robustness and Research Implications}
\subsection{Observation and Selection Sensitivities}
The stored repository key disagrees with a lexical URL parse for 60 positive observations. Excluding them removes 24 repository endpoints (7,171 to 7,147); greedy at 100 then gives $C=5{,}524$ and $P=799$. We discard those flags instead of assigning them to corrected URLs, which would require fresh observation.

Removing bundled-context nodes preserves every workflow-repository source set and all 462,170 identity--repository pairs. Repository $C$, $H$, and $P$ therefore remain unchanged for fixed selections. At the workflow-package level, one endpoint loses 28 incidence pairs; repository deduplication absorbs that change. The invariance is specific to the repository endpoint definition.

Reversing tie order yields $C=5{,}537$ and $P=793$. Excluding the 1, 5, or 10 largest portfolios gives coverage of 5,522, 5,149, and 5,076. Grouping equal stored email hashes as a hypothetical control unit yields 12,147 groups with greedy coverage at 5,538. These tests reflect eligibility sensitivity rather than verified credential control.

\subsection{Hybrid, Threshold, and Efficacy Results}
The equal-weight hybrid reaches $C=5{,}020$ (70.0\%), $H=31.19\%$, and $P=626$ at budget 100. The lexicographic exclusion heuristic reaches the same coverage with $P=1{,}022$, but $H$ falls to $13.23\%$. Increasing completed source sets thus has a measurable exposure tradeoff on this graph.

For $k=2$, $T_2(\varnothing)=1{,}716$ across 7,158 source-reachable endpoints. Greedy, Exact Union, and Hybrid yield $T_2=2{,}112$, $2{,}137$, and $2{,}153$, respectively, giving increments $\Delta T_2=396$, $421$, and $437$. These baseline-adjusted residual counts differ from strict zero-anchored counts: $P_2^{\rm strict}=1{,}145$, $793$, and $961$, respectively. Neither definition measures a deployed multisigner policy.

At assumed efficacy $\eta=0.90$, Exact Union gives $H_\eta=30.41\%$ and Hybrid $28.07\%$, versus Greedy's unattenuated $11.48\%$. A common multiplier preserves $H$ rankings and does not model correlated CI failures.
