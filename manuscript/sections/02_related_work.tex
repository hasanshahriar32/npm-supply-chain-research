\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Maintainer concentration and prioritization.}
Zimmermann et al.~\cite{zimmermann2019} define maintainer reach as a union over maintained packages, formulate greedy maintainer coverage, and study maintainer vetting. These establish the central reach and coverage techniques used here. Zahan et al.~\cite{zahan2022} examine npm weak-link signals, including maintainer characteristics. Wang's recent preprint~\cite{wang2026} evaluates centrality-based package-node hardening using cascade simulations. Our experimental unit is an observed maintainer username, and our intervention removes only the selected account-origin scenario. It does not delete a package or halt traversal through a hardened downstream package. We extend these studies by jointly reporting coverage, additive scenario reduction, and all-source exclusion under the same selections.

\textbf{Coverage and dependency semantics.}
Cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization and the greedy approximation guarantee are established~\cite{nemhauser1978}. Our exact-union ranking optimizes a separate modular objective under equal scenario weights and costs. Neither algorithmic property is a novelty claim. Liu et al.~\cite{liu2022} show the importance of npm resolution semantics for dependency analysis. We accordingly evaluate package projection against version-continuous paths while distinguishing an observed union graph from a resolved installation.

\textbf{Workflow and temporal evidence.}
Workflow security characterization~\cite{koishybayev2022} and ARGUS taint analysis~\cite{muralee2023} examine configuration or data flow beyond directory presence. Our flag identifies associated endpoints without equivalent evidence of execution or secret access. npm-follower~\cite{pinckney2023} and CHRONO-RESOLUTION~\cite{rahman2026} support archival and release-point analysis; PatchRisk~\cite{patchrisk2026} studies future vulnerability exposure. These motivate temporal follow-up, but our cross-sectional experiment evaluates structural objectives rather than forecasting compromise. Our literature search through 20 October 2026 positions the contribution as this measured extension, without claiming the first npm graph analysis or forecasting method.
