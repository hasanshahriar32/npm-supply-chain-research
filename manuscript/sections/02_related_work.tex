\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Maintainer prioritization.} Zimmermann et al.~\cite{zimmermann2019} established maintainer-union reach, greedy coverage, and maintainer vetting. Zahan et al.~\cite{zahan2022} study npm weak links; Wang~\cite{wang2026} evaluates package-node hardening in cascade simulations. We select observed usernames and remove only their account-origin scenarios, without deleting packages or blocking other origins. Coverage's submodular greedy guarantee is established~\cite{nemhauser1978}; exact-union ranking instead optimizes modular exposure under equal weights and costs.

\textbf{Dependency semantics.} Liu et al.~\cite{liu2022} and He et al.~\cite{he2025} motivate resolution-aware analysis. Pillar disclosed OpenCode's legacy npm \texttt{open} command-injection path (its maintainer \texttt{sindresorhus} ranks first by exact union in our graph) on Windows/WSL on October 19; the dependency fix shipped September 28 without a CVE~\cite{opencode2026}. This motivates version-aware inventory beyond CVE matching. Shen et al.~\cite{gao2025emse} report 81.26\% false positives for package-level findings under finer analysis in their studied corpus; this is not an npm-wide rate. Jayasuriya et al.~\cite{jayasuriya2024} examine version-breaking changes in Maven. Neither package nor version reach establishes executed vulnerable code.

\textbf{Workflow and authority evidence.} Workflow characterization~\cite{koishybayev2022}, ARGUS~\cite{muralee2023}, and unused-dependency CI waste~\cite{weeraddana2024} distinguish workflow association from execution. Kalu et al.~\cite{okafor2025} document technical, organizational, and human barriers to software signing. Repository accessibility~\cite{tsakpinis2024}, npm-follower~\cite{pinckney2023}, and release-point reconstruction~\cite{rahman2026} motivate explicit observation and temporal limits. Our cross-sectional study measures structural objectives rather than forecasting compromise.
