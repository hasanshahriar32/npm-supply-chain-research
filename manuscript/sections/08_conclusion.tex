\section{Conclusion}
Our npm study measures a substantial tradeoff between distinct downstream coverage and additive exposure reduction under a fixed account-origin model. At 100 identities, greedy covers 5,538 workflow repositories but excludes every observed source for only 800; exact-union ranking yields greater conditional exposure reduction. Version continuity reduces total exposure pairs by 42.44\% while leaving aggregate greedy coverage comparatively stable. These measurements extend established maintainer-reach methods with explicit objective and representation comparisons. The completed prospective acquisition pilot provides a frozen starting point for outcome research; pending observations support no forecast-performance claim.
