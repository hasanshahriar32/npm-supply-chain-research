\section{Conclusion}
Independent reconstruction reproduces seven database metric fields, yet its companion CSV disagrees on workflow reach and score for 17,809 packages. The default top-253 queues share 65 entries under a stated tie rule. Matching package populations therefore do not imply matching review decisions. The incident companion lacks eligible controls, leaving prediction and calibration unevaluated. Artifact consistency and historical outcome eligibility should precede model comparison; our audit quantifies the consequences of failing that prerequisite.
