\subsection{Use of AI Assistance}
AI assistance was used for manuscript drafting and revision, code revision, analysis checks, and source verification, including assistance through OpenAI's Codex interface. This use extended beyond spelling and grammar correction. AI-generated text and suggestions are not observational evidence: numerical claims are tied to retained data and executable consistency checks, and literature claims require their cited sources. The authors retain responsibility for the final manuscript, interpretation, code, and references.
