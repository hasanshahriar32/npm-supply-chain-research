\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

We position the study against work on maintainer allocation, dependency structure and consumer-side measurement.

\subsection{Ecosystem Structure and Centrality-Based Prioritization}
Decan et al. measured how dependency concentration evolved across seven package ecosystems~\cite{decan2019}. In the present study, concentration motivates examining the distribution of reachable repositories across maintainer accounts. We compare three objectives at identical account budgets and measure the cost of optimizing one objective in units of another.

\subsection{Developer allocation and consumer evidence}
Schueller and Wachs model how developer departures and dependency relationships jointly affect libraries in the Rust ecosystem and compare allocations of limited developer support~\cite{schueller2024}. Our empirical question concerns how a fixed npm account selection changes across graph representations and how much of its downstream reach can be traced in consumer artifacts. Decan et al. compare dependency-network evolution across ecosystems~\cite{decan2019}. Zerouali et al. distinguish dependents distributed through registries from external GitHub consumers~\cite{zerouali2022}. We draw a separate application sample because a package's development repository and an application that consumes the package supply evidence about different relationships.

Samaana et al. vary dependency depth in an npm replication and find changes in the magnitude of measured technical leverage~\cite{samaana2025}. Chinthanet et al. distinguish upstream fixing releases from downstream adoption~\cite{chinthanet2021}, and Alfadel et al. investigate what vulnerability information was discoverable at different points in time~\cite{alfadel2023}. These studies motivate preserving dependency versions, consumer identity and observation dates in each comparison. Mohayeji et al. measure how developers respond to dependency updates~\cite{mohayeji2025}. Our outcome is the presence of a documented dependency path at a pinned commit. Observing how developers act on a review recommendation would require a separate study.
