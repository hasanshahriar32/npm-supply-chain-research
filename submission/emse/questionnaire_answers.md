**Journal-first contributions**

This manuscript reports previously unpublished empirical results on maintainer prioritization in npm supply chains. It compares distinct repository coverage, account-origin exposure and all-source exclusion on the same retained graph and evaluates the sensitivity of these results to graph representation, endpoint definitions, scalarization and assumed heterogeneous weights. It also examines the limits of bounded historical reconstruction and registry metadata for establishing release authority. The work distinguishes structural prioritization from measured compromise probabilities or deployed-control effects. It does not claim a new greedy coverage algorithm.
