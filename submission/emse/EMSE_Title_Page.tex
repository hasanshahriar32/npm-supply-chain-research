The study analyzes public software-ecosystem metadata and does not involve recruited human participants, interventions on individuals, or execution of package payloads. No participant consent was collected. Usernames are used as observed graph identifiers, without inferring misconduct or present-day account security. The prospective observations contain no measured incident outcomes at the time of this manuscript.
